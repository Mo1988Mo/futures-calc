# TradeSmart Co. — Support Guide

## Getting Started
1. Open the Futures Calculator at tradesmart.co/calc
2. Select Long or Short position
3. Enter your Entry Price, Leverage, and Margin
4. Add SL/TP levels for risk analysis
5. Hit CALCULATE to see full position breakdown

## Common Issues

**Q: Why is my PnL showing negative even with correct prices?**
Check your fee settings. Taker fees on both entry and close can
significantly reduce net PnL, especially on small moves.

**Q: Liquidation price seems wrong?**
Our calculator uses a simplified model. Actual liquidation prices
vary by exchange and margin mode (cross vs isolated).

**Q: Funding rate — what value should I enter?**
Check your exchange's current funding rate. Binance typically
shows this on the perpetual contract page. Default is 0.01%/8h.
