# TradeSmart Co. — Leave & Work Policy

## Working Hours
- Core hours: 09:00–18:00 (GMT+3:30)
- Remote-first company; flexible start times allowed

## Annual Leave
- All full-time employees receive 20 days paid leave per year
- Leave must be requested at least 5 business days in advance
- Unused leave rolls over (max 10 days)

## Sick Leave
- Up to 10 days per year with medical documentation
- No documentation required for absences under 2 days

## Public Holidays
- TradeSmart observes all Iranian national holidays
- Trading desk staff receive compensatory days for holiday coverage

## Emergency Leave
- Up to 3 days per year for family emergencies
- Manager approval required within 24 hours
