# TradeSmart Co. — Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is TradeSmart Co.?**
TradeSmart Co. is a futures trading analytics platform helping traders calculate risk, PnL, and position sizing in real time.

**Q: What markets does the platform support?**
We support USDT-margined perpetual futures on major exchanges including Binance, Bybit, and OKX.

**Q: How is PnL calculated?**
PnL = (Close Price - Entry Price) × Contract Quantity, adjusted for maker/taker fees and funding rate costs.

**Q: What is the funding rate?**
Funding rate is a periodic fee exchanged between long and short holders in perpetual contracts, typically every 8 hours.

**Q: Is leverage safe to use?**
Higher leverage increases both potential gains and liquidation risk. We recommend 5x–10x for most traders.

**Q: How do I calculate my liquidation price?**
Our calculator estimates liquidation as: Entry ± (Entry / Leverage) − (Entry × Maintenance Margin Rate).
